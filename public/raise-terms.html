The Tut™ Raise · AGNT Inner Circle

The deal, plainly.

This is a community Kickstarter — not an investment. You're helping kickstart Tut™ and AGNT to the next level. You're not buying a stake and you shouldn't expect a profit. By contributing, you agree to everything below.
1

What you get

A soulbound (non-transferable) on-chain receipt for your contribution, plus Inner Circle access, an AGNTS PFP, and $AGNTS access. These are things we're building toward on a best-effort basis — not guaranteed to launch, last, or hold any value.

2

How long it counts

Your receipt is recognized as a contribution for one year, or until we hit our build benchmarks — activations across the Tut™ × AGNT ecosystem — whichever comes first. After that it lives on-chain forever as proof you were here early.

3

It's final

Contributions are on-chain, which means no refunds and no take-backs. Double-check the amount and network (Ethereum mainnet) before you confirm.

4

How much

$250 minimum, $10,000 maximum per person. Want to do more? DM @Tuteth_.

5

Know the risk

Crypto is risky and early projects can fail. The token, the PFP, and AGNT itself might never launch or might go to zero. Smart contracts can have bugs. Only contribute what you're completely fine losing. Nothing here is financial advice.

6

The basics

You're 18+, using your own funds, and not somewhere this isn't allowed. Your X handle and Discord are self-attested and go public + permanent on-chain — don't enter anything you wouldn't want out there forever.
